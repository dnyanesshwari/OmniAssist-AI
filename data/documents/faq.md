# QuickCare India — Frequently Asked Questions

## Which cities do you serve?
We currently serve Delhi, Mumbai, Pune, Bengaluru and Hyderabad. We do not yet operate in other cities.

## Are your technicians verified?
Yes. Every technician completes a background check and skills training before joining QuickCare, and carries a QuickCare ID card.

## Do I need to be at home during the service?
An adult must be present at the start of the service to show the work area and to approve any quote for spare parts.

## How do I book an appointment?
Choose a service, city and time slot in chat or by voice. The slot is held for 10 minutes and the booking is confirmed once you say yes.

## What if I need a service outside working hours?
Service hours are 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM IST. We do not offer late-night or emergency visits at present.

## How do I raise a complaint?
Complaints about safety, damage or technician behaviour are handled by a human agent. Ask for a human agent in chat or by voice and a ticket is created with a summary of your conversation.

## Is my data safe?
We use your name, phone number and address only to deliver your booking. We do not sell customer data.
