# QuickCare India — Services and Pricing

QuickCare India provides trained home-service professionals in Delhi, Mumbai, Pune, Bengaluru and Hyderabad. Service hours are 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM IST, every day of the week.

## Price list
All prices are in Indian Rupees (INR) and include GST unless stated otherwise.
- AC Service & Cleaning: ₹599 per unit, about 60 minutes.
- AC Repair: ₹499 visit and diagnosis charge, about 60 minutes. Spare parts are charged extra.
- Plumbing Repair: ₹349 visit charge, about 60 minutes. Parts are charged extra.
- Electrician Visit: ₹299 visit charge, about 30 minutes. Parts are charged extra.
- Washing Machine and Fridge Repair: ₹449 visit and diagnosis charge, about 60 minutes. Parts are charged extra.
- Home Deep Cleaning: ₹2,499 for homes up to 2 BHK, about 3 hours.
- Pest Control: ₹1,299 per treatment, about 90 minutes.

## Parts and extra charges
The technician shares a quote for spare parts before starting any repair. Parts are only installed after the customer approves the quote. The visit charge is adjusted against the repair bill if the customer approves the repair.

## Payment
Payment is collected after the service is completed. We accept UPI, debit and credit cards, and cash.
