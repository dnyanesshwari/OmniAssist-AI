# QuickCare India — Warranty Policy

## Service warranty
Repair services carry a 30-day service warranty. If the same fault returns within 30 days of the service date, QuickCare will send a technician to fix it at no extra visit charge.
AC Service & Cleaning carries a 7-day re-clean guarantee for cleaning quality issues.
Home Deep Cleaning and Pest Control carry a 15-day follow-up guarantee: QuickCare will repeat the affected area or treatment once at no charge if the issue persists.

## Parts warranty
Spare parts supplied by QuickCare carry a 90-day warranty against manufacturing defects. Parts supplied by the customer are not covered.

## What is not covered
- Damage caused by misuse, accidents, water or power surges.
- Repairs or modifications made by anyone other than a QuickCare technician after our service.
- Faults unrelated to the original repair.
- Normal wear and tear and consumables.

## How to claim
Contact support with your booking reference. Warranty claims are verified against the service date and the work recorded by the technician.
