# QuickCare India — Booking and Cancellation Policy

## Booking rules
Appointments start on the hour or half hour between 8:00 AM and 8:00 PM IST. The whole service must finish by 8:00 PM. Bookings need at least 2 hours' notice.
A selected slot is held for 10 minutes. The booking is only confirmed after the customer explicitly confirms it. Unconfirmed holds expire automatically.

## Cancellation charges
Cancellation charges depend on how far ahead of the appointment start time the customer cancels.
- More than 24 hours before the appointment: free cancellation, no charge.
- Between 2 and 24 hours before the appointment: 20% of the service price.
- Less than 2 hours before the appointment: 50% of the service price.
Cancelling a slot that is still on hold (not yet confirmed) is always free.

## Rescheduling
To change the time of a booking, cancel it and make a new booking. The same cancellation charges apply to the cancelled booking.

## Technician no-show
If the technician does not arrive within 30 minutes of the appointment start, the customer can request a free rebooking or a full refund of any advance payment by contacting a human agent.
